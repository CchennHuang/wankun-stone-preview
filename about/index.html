FAQ

Before we start

How is production capacity assessed for my project?+

Share the drawings, material, quantities and required dates. The factory reviews the fabrication requirements and production plan for that specific order.

What will I be able to review remotely?+

Project information includes relevant drawings, material and layout views, progress updates, dry-lay images and inspection details. The sharing method and checkpoints are agreed for your project.

How are quality and warranty arrangements handled?+

Inspection criteria, acceptance requirements, responsibilities and applicable warranty terms are confirmed for each order. Alice coordinates quality questions and factory follow-up.

What should I send for a project in Singapore or Malaysia?+

Send your drawings or piece schedule, project location, material, approximate quantity and required dates. Add any packaging, labelling or technical requirements you already have.
