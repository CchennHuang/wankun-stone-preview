PROCESS EVIDENCE / MATERIAL & LAYOUT

A real slab becomes a layout you can review.

Every slab has its own natural pattern. Scanning captures the actual material, QR-linked records identify the slabs, and digital layout helps plan the pattern before the physical dry lay.

Independent process references, rather than before-and-after views of one project.

  1. 01

    Actual slab scan

    Capture the real pattern and material information.

  2. 02

    Layout review

    Plan the arrangement using the scanned slabs.

  3. 03

    Factory dry lay

    Lay out the finished pieces and review the overall effect.

  4. 04

    Numbered pieces

    Identify each component for installation in the confirmed arrangement.

02 / QUALITY ASSURANCE

Define the requirements before production. Review the material and layout, follow fabrication checks, and see the dry lay before dispatch.

QUALITY RESPONSIBILITY & FOLLOW-UP

Quality questions have a clear route.

Agree the inspection and acceptance criteria, quality responsibilities and applicable warranty terms for your order. If a concern arises, share the piece reference and supporting details with Alice for factory review and follow-up.
